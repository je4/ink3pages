## PARTOUT

PARTOUT is a project of d PANCH – Performance Art Network CH and the closing project of [PANCH EUROVISION] (https://panch.li/2018-2020eurovision/). 
the estate contains interviews and broad documentation of LIVE ART WORKSHOPs and ACTS OF TRANSMISSION.
Both were recorded in intense collaborations in Lausanne and Basel, which were semi-publicly open. 
The project was complemented by athrough a cooperation framework with European Artists which were invited by the Partout/Eurovision funding.
The collection provides remarkable insights in collaboration practices in contemporary performing arts.
